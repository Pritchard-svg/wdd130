Our Zambezi Adventures

Whether you are stepping into a raft for the first time or chasing the ultimate adrenaline rush, we have a journey crafted for you. Every trip is guided by our experienced Zambezi specialists who bring both safety and storytelling to every stretch of the river.

The Sunrise Float

🌞 Beginner • Half Day • All Ages Welcome

Start your Zambezi story gently. The Sunrise Float takes you through the calmer upper stretches of the river as the African sun rises over the gorge. You will drift past basking hippos, soaring fish eagles, and ancient canyon walls painted gold by the morning light. No experience needed — just a sense of wonder and a willingness to get a little wet.

  • Duration: 4 hours
  • Difficulty: Grade 1–2
  • Group Size: Up to 12 people
  • Includes: Life jacket, paddle, light breakfast, guide
  • Price: $65 per person

The Gorge Runner

🌊 Intermediate • Full Day • Ages 16+

The Gorge Runner is our most popular full-day trip — a breathtaking journey through the heart of the Batoka Gorge. You will paddle through a mix of calm stretches and exciting Grade 3–4 rapids with names like "The Washing Machine" and "Ghostrider." Between the rapids, your guide will share stories of the river's history and folklore. Lunch is served riverside under a Zambezi acacia tree.

  • Duration: 8 hours
  • Difficulty: Grade 3–4
  • Group Size: Up to 10 people
  • Includes: Full safety gear, riverside lunch, guide, photos
  • Price: $130 per person

The Zambezi Extreme

🔥 Advanced • Full Day • Ages 18+ • Fitness Required

This is the real thing. The Zambezi Extreme takes you through some of the most powerful commercial white-water rapids on the planet — including the infamous Grade 5 rapid known as "Oblivion." You will flip, swim, and laugh your way through a day that will live in your memory forever. Our expert guides and safety kayakers are with you every stroke of the way.

  • Duration: 9 hours
  • Difficulty: Grade 4–5
  • Group Size: Up to 8 people
  • Includes: Full safety gear, rescue kayakers, lunch, guide, video footage
  • Price: $185 per person
